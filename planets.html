Earth is one of eight planets that orbit the Sun. Each planet is different in size, temperature, and appearance.

The Eight Planets

Planet Position from Sun Type Interesting Fact
Mercury 1st Rocky It is the closest planet to the Sun.
Venus 2nd Rocky It is the hottest planet in our solar system.
Earth 3rd Rocky It is the only known planet with life.
Mars 4th Rocky It is known as the Red Planet.
Jupiter 5th Gas Giant It is the largest planet in our solar system.
Saturn 6th Gas Giant It is famous for its beautiful rings.
Uranus 7th Ice Giant It rotates almost on its side.
Neptune 8th Ice Giant It is the farthest planet from the Sun.

Earth: Our Home 🌍

Earth has liquid water, an atmosphere, and conditions that allow many different forms of life to exist.
