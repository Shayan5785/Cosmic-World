What Is the Universe?

The universe contains everything that exists, including galaxies, stars, planets, moons, gas, dust, and even space itself.

Did You Know?

Our home, Earth, is part of a solar system that contains the Sun and eight planets.

  • The Sun is a star.
  • Earth is the third planet from the Sun.
  • The Moon is Earth's natural satellite.
  • Our solar system is inside the Milky Way galaxy.
